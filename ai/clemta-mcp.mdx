The Clemta MCP server lets an agent work with your Clemta account. Ask in
plain words, and the agent answers from your own companies:

- Which invoices are still unpaid, and from whom?
- What did we spend on software last quarter?
- What came into the business checking account this week?
- What does the profit and loss look like for March?
- Draft an invoice to Acme for 20 hours of consulting.

It is for people using the Clemta app, about their own companies. Partner
teams have their own server: see [Partner MCP server](/ai/partner-mcp).

This is not the [docs search server](/ai/coding-agents#docs-search-mcp), which
searches these pages and calls nothing.

## Connect

The server is at:

```text
https://mcp.clemta.com
```

Add it to your client. There is no key to paste: the first time the client
connects, it opens Clemta in your browser, and you approve the connection
there.

## What it can see

What you approve decides what the agent gets. Each area is a separate
permission on the approval screen:

| Area | What the agent can look up |
|---|---|
| Company | Your companies and their details |
| Sales | Invoices, credit notes, contacts, revenue accounts |
| Purchases | Bills and vendor credits |
| Banking | Bank accounts, transactions, payouts |
| Accounting | Balance sheet, profit and loss, cash flow, trial balance, general ledger, journal entries, periods and fiscal years |

An area you decline is not there at all: the agent does not see its tools and
does not try them. The same goes for an area your plan does not include. A
company without bookkeeping, for example, gets no accounting tools.

## What it can change

Two things, both under the sales permission:

- **Draft an invoice.** The draft is not sent and not issued. It has no
  invoice number and nobody is emailed. The agent gives you a link, and you
  review and send it in Clemta yourself.
- **Create a contact.** A contact is created right away, so you can invoice
  it. It sends no email, and you can edit or delete it in Clemta.

Nothing else. The agent cannot send or void an invoice, pay a bill, move
money, or change your books.

## Prompts

Claude shows three ready-made prompts in its slash menu once the server is
connected:

- `draft-invoice` drafts an invoice to a contact.
- `monthly-summary` reads back what was invoiced, paid and still outstanding
  for a month.
- `financial-summary` covers profit and loss, balance sheet and cash for a
  period.

Other clients see the same tools, but may not show the prompts.
